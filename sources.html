Sources & Notes

Where the Story Comes From

Every name and date on this site comes from a real record. Here they are, in plain language.

A note on the 9th Infantry

William’s discharge says “Co. E, 9th Inf.” — Company E of the 9th Infantry Regiment, part of the 2nd Infantry Division. It is easy to confuse with the 9th Infantry Division, a different unit that fought at Cherbourg. This site follows the regiment.

Spellings

Census-takers wrote names down as they heard them. Abagail appears as Abigal and Abby; Washington as “K. W.”; Melcena as Melsinia and Melzena; Nora as “Tinna”; Bernadean as Bernadine. Ages can differ by a year or two from one record to the next.

William’s children

William and Bertha raised eleven children, as Cecil Jr.’s and Hershel’s obituaries record. William’s 2001 obituary also remembers his son William Ray, who died before him.

Living family

Living members of the family appear by first name and family role only.

The Dragging Canoe legend

The story of kinship with Dragging Canoe’s daughter is a family legend, shared on this site as a legend. Dragging Canoe himself is well documented; the history of his family is drawn from the Tennessee Encyclopedia and the work of E. Raymond Evans.
